This browser can't record with Reel. Use an up-to-date Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on Windows or macOS.

Install Reel Recorder

In Chrome or Edge, choose Install app from the address bar or browser menu. On a Mac, you can also open Recorder in Safari and choose File → Add to Dock.

You can also record here without installing. Keep Reel open until uploads finish. Shortcuts work while Reel or its floating controls are focused; the browser asks you what to share for each new recording.

New recording

After you press Start, pick what to share: your entire screen, a window or a browser tab. Tick Also share system audio (screen) or Also share tab audio (tab) to include sound from your computer.

To include sound from the tab, choose a system audio option below and tick Also share tab audio in the browser picker.

Sign in to select a calendar invitation for team recaps.

Records video, your microphone and computer sound. For Google Meet or Teams in a browser, share the meeting tab and tick Also share tab audio. For Zoom or Teams desktop, share your screen with system audio. Keep this Reel window open until transcription finishes.

Meeting audio is sent securely to OpenAI for transcription and AI meeting notes. Organization sign-in is required. The video stays in this browser unless you choose Share video. When signed in, its title and date appear in your private Meetings portal.

New meeting notes are automatically saved in Notion. Recaps go to internal calendar invitees, including nonattendees; external invitees are excluded. A manual meeting without an invitation sends a recap only to you.
